# Wishlist

Everything batchmates have asked for, copied from our WhatsApp group. I never
got to any of it. Some of it is great, some of it is a lot. — Zara

1. **"Can it help me prep for an interview?"** — Hira
   > Like, I paste the job post and my CV, and it tells me what they'll
   > probably ask and which parts of my CV to talk about. And shows me where
   > it got that from, so I know it's not making it up.
2. **Logins.** "I don't want to run my own copy. Can't we all just log in?" — Danish
3. **Follow-up reminders.** "Remind me to follow up if I haven't heard back in
   a week." — Mahnoor
4. **Which CV did I send?** Attach the CV version you sent to each application.
5. **Salary.** Track the salary range and what they offered.
6. **Import from email.** Read application confirmations from Gmail
   automatically.
7. **Rejection reasons.** Note why you were rejected, so you can spot patterns.
8. **Mobile.** "It's unusable on my phone." — everyone
9. **Dark mode.**
10. **Stats.** How many applications turn into interviews?
11. **Referrals.** Track who referred you, so you can thank them.
12. **Share with a mentor.** A read-only link for a mentor or a parent.
13. **Calendar.** Put interviews in Google Calendar.
14. **Company notes.** What you learned about each company.

Things that already bother me:

- Everyone runs their own copy. Updates mean everyone pulls again.
- Everything lives in one JSON file. Two tabs open at once can overwrite each
  other.
- It's ugly. I know.
